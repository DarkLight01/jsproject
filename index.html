How I Started Learning HTML

12 Sep 2026 • 3 min read

HTML looked confusing at first — all those tags. Here's the simple routine that got me to my first page.

I started with just the p, img and a tags and built one tiny page every day after watching YouTube tutorials. My rule: practice 1 hour daily, don't try to memorize everything. Build small, break things, fix them — that's how it clicks. Once structure made sense, CSS suddenly felt much easier.

My Trip to Manali

28 Aug 2026 • 4 min read

Snow, freezing nights, and a 3-day budget trip with friends — here's the full breakdown.

We took an overnight bus from Delhi, split a cozy homestay, and spent 3 days chasing snow viewpoints and hot maggi. Total came to around ₹6000 per person including stay, food and local travel. My tip: go in a group of 4-6, book transport together, and carry proper winter layers — it gets really cold after sunset.

Best Street Food in Delhi

15 Aug 2026 • 3 min read

Chole bhature, momos, golgappe — where to eat well in Delhi for just ₹200-300.

Chandni Chowk is my top pick for a food walk: aloo tikki, chole bhature, golgappe and jalebi within a few lanes. Most plates are ₹80-150, so you can eat like a king for ₹200-300. Go hungry, carry cash, start early to beat the crowd, and don't skip the fresh lime soda between stops.

What is JavaScript?

02 Aug 2026 • 5 min read

The language that makes buttons, search and forms actually work — explained simply.

JavaScript handles clicks, form validation, and showing or hiding content without reloading the page. I started with getElementById, functions, if-else and loops. It felt hard for a week, then practice made it click. This very blog uses it for live search, filters, likes and comments — open the console and try things out.
